# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The admin portal is used by Tarryn and trusted admin staff managing the Business of Happiness mobile app. They are in operational workflows: responding to users, managing course access, publishing announcements, moderating community content, and maintaining course materials.

## Product Purpose

The admin webapp replaces direct Supabase administration with focused, safer workflows for the app's five v1 admin capabilities: Tarryn inbox, content management, whitelist management, announcements, and moderation. Success means admins can complete routine operations without touching Supabase tables directly.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, supportive. The interface should feel trustworthy and low-friction, with the admin tool disappearing behind the task rather than calling attention to itself.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboard tropes, emoji, over-styled cards, heavy gradients, novelty controls, and anything that makes routine admin work feel slower or less reliable. Do not use mobile-first compromises for the admin portal; it is desktop-first by spec.

## Design Principles

- Keep admin workflows direct and legible.
- Prefer safe defaults for destructive or notification-triggering actions.
- Make errors actionable and visible at the point of work.
- Preserve the shared Business of Happiness color system and centralize user-facing copy.
- Favor familiar product UI patterns over visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use accessible form labels, keyboard-reachable controls, visible focus states, and readable contrast. Respect reduced motion where motion is introduced. All copy is English only and must avoid emoji.
